# Concept exploration

50 low-cost structured concepts are retained in concept-catalog.json. These are hypotheses, not 50 polished logos. The three basic silhouettes were constructed in monochrome before colour application.

Generated nouns: joint, beam, bracket, frame, gate, rail, step, bridge, ledger, ruler, tile, hinge, keystone, thread, anchor, compass, harbour, relay, workbench, path.

Designer-selected working nouns (not user-approved): joint, beam, bracket, frame, gate, rail, step, bridge, ledger, ruler, tile, hinge, keystone, thread, anchor. Structural nouns describe implementation, boundaries, repeatability and ownership. Compass, harbour, relay, workbench and path were deprioritised because they add navigation, logistics or workshop associations.

Four territories: assembled engineering; editorial evidence; bounded delivery; measured progress.

Shortlist: C01, C02, C05, C06, C07, C11, C12, C16, C21, C26. Scores in the catalog are heuristic, not market evidence.

Developed: A/C01 Release Joint; B/C06 Open Frame; C/C11 Working Grid. A combines a stable elbow with a separated upper component; B uses enclosure; C uses asymmetric rhythm.

Recommendation A has the clearest reusable geometry. B risks looking like a letter E or an interface icon. C risks a generic dashboard/grid association. A also has resemblance risk: it needs a proper competitor comparison before final adoption.

## Founder selection

On 9 October 2026 the founder selected C/C11, **03 / The Working Grid**. This supersedes the initial recommendation A. Its ordered modules suit the desired minimalist direction. Keep A and B as exploration history. The grid association and competitor resemblance checks remain open risks. All current primary assets now use C.
