# reem.solutions — quick identity draft

Status: direction 03 selected by founder; production refinement pending. Mode: name-to-brand. Date: 9 October 2026.

## Foundation
For UK SaaS founders, CTOs and engineering leads with an existing AI workload, reem.solutions provides AI production engineering: a clear release path, agreed acceptance evidence and an operable handover. Agency partnerships are secondary.

Transformation: from uncertainty about launch and ownership to a system the client can understand and operate. Brand role: accountable engineering partner. Emotional benefit: confidence grounded in visible work. Promise: make production readiness inspectable. Reasons to believe are proposed delivery mechanisms from the brief—named ownership, weekly demos, evaluations and runbooks—not verified customer outcomes.

Personality: precise, calm, candid, practical. Technical depth with approachable language; restraint with enough character to be remembered. Avoid inflated AI claims, playful mascots, futuristic effects and claims of guaranteed reliability. Alternatives include internal delivery, general development agencies and separate infrastructure contractors; differentiation is a positioning hypothesis, not a researched competitive claim.

Maturity: first service landing-page brief. Future expansion and existing brand recognition are unknown. Assume no established identity equity and retain the supplied name. The UK reference guides tone and visual restraint; it does not imply a UK office.

## Name fit
Retain with descriptor: **reem.solutions — AI production engineering**. Use the full lowercase name in navigation and formal first references; “reem” may follow in prose. Pronunciation assumed “reem”; confirm if different. “Solutions” is broad, so the descriptor must appear early. No invented etymology. Domain ownership, naming associations and trademark clearance have not been checked.

## Selected direction: The Working Grid
A vertical spine and two stacked blocks communicate order, modularity and repeatable delivery. The mark is secondary to a readable wordmark; its story need not be explained to customers. Keep the two open channels and the unequal block heights. Do not rearrange the modules or add a surrounding circle.

The noun-to-logo exploration uses the skill's visual method. Positioning, tone and service-system decisions are added strategy work.

## Palette and type
Ink #202522, Paper #F4F3EE, White #FFFFFF, Cobalt #244DD8. Muted copy #626862; rule #D2D5CF. Use paper or white for most surfaces, ink for text, cobalt for actions and occasional labels. Rule colour is decorative and must not be the sole boundary of form controls.

Arial/Helvetica is the practical draft sans-serif. Use regular body copy and bold labels/wordmark; avoid fake medium weights where unavailable. Hero 40–72 px, body 18 px with 1.6 line height. Sentence case, short headlines and left-aligned text. Monospace is reserved for genuine technical labels and numbered process steps. No paid typeface required by this draft.

Wordmarks contain editable live text. They are draft lockups, not outlined master artwork; final kerning and outlines are a production step during production refinement.

## Layout and visual DNA
Use a 1200 px content container, 24 px mobile gutters and 48 px desktop gutters. Spacing follows 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96/128 px. Prefer horizontal rules and generous space over repeated bordered cards. Controls use a 3 px radius; panels remain square. On mobile, stack content in reading order.

Repeat the mark's aligned modules and open channels sparingly in diagrams and section transitions. Icons use consistent orthogonal strokes with open counters. Illustration should explain a release path or operating system. Any example must be labelled; never fabricate dashboard results. Data graphics require text labels, not colour alone. A documentation cover can use the mark, title, workload and date. A social tile can use a plain service statement on ink, with the white mark.

Motion: 160 ms ease-out for functional state transitions; respect reduced motion. No ambient logo animation. These are a service firm's relevant touchpoints; app and browser-extension product systems are not currently needed.

## Voice
Use specific engineering nouns and observable deliverables. Preserve “Ship AI that holds up in production.” and “Discuss your project.” Prefer “Review test results and failure paths” to vague quality claims. Use UK English. Do not add testimonials, numerical outcomes, certifications, offices or continuous support claims.

## Logo usage
Mark geometry uses a 64-unit artboard; the spine is 16 units wide, the right blocks are 24 units wide, and the open channels are 8 units wide. Clear space: at least 16 units outside the artboard when used alone, or the same scaled unit around a lockup. Minimum standalone digital size: provisionally 16 px, pending actual-size raster inspection. Prefer at least 24 px in navigation. Minimum horizontal lockup width: 180 px; below that, use the symbol with an accessible brand name.

Use ink on paper/white and white on ink. One-colour variants are provided. Never stretch, add shadows, close the gaps or put cobalt on ink. An engraving approximation may use the black mark at 12 mm wide; embroidery requires vendor sampling before specifying a minimum.
