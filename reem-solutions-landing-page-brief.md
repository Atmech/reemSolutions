# reem.solutions landing page content and build context

Purpose: give a designer or coding agent the service context, customer-facing copy and functional requirements needed to build the first UK-market landing page. Its primary conversion is a qualified project conversation.

## 1. Service context

**Company name:** reem.solutions.

**Category:** AI production engineering.

**Positioning sentence:** reem.solutions helps SaaS teams turn working AI prototypes into reliable live products through deployment, evaluation and ongoing operations.

**Primary reader:** a UK SaaS founder, CTO or engineering lead with an existing AI feature, prototype or workload. Their launch is approaching, their engineers are stretched, or their live system needs better reliability and cost visibility.

**Secondary reader:** a software agency that needs an engineering partner for client deployment and operations. Give this audience one short section without diluting the SaaS focus.

**Buying moments:** preparing for the first real users; struggling with slow or inconsistent responses; missing release tests; unclear cloud/model costs; needing a dependable owner after launch.

**The promise:** a clear path to production, a named technical owner, evidence at acceptance and a system the client can continue to operate.

## 2. Build prompt

Build a responsive landing page for reem.solutions, an AI production engineering service for UK SaaS teams. The page should help an engineering buyer recognise their launch problem, understand the deliverables and request a project conversation.

Use the copy blocks in section 3 as the content source. Keep builder instructions, internal commercial assumptions and launch checks out of the rendered page. Structure the page in this order: hero, production problems, deliverables, engagements, delivery process, agency partners, FAQs and project enquiry.

The main CTA is “Discuss your project”. Until an actual booking destination is supplied, it should scroll to the project enquiry form. The secondary CTA is “See how we work” and scrolls to the delivery section. Service CTAs should open the same form with the relevant engagement selected. Never invent a booking URL or contact inbox.

There are no verified testimonials, client logos or outcome statistics supplied for this service. Show tangible deliverables and the process instead. Any illustrative example must be labelled as an example and must not be presented as a case study. Do not publish internal revenue targets, margins, founder allocation, sales quotas or incorporation planning. Do not claim a UK office, certifications, guaranteed compliance, model-provider partnership, continuous support or guaranteed launch date.

Use the commercial copy below as a draft pricing configuration. The internal plan treats these prices and capacity levels as hypotheses; the founder must settle them before launch. They are suitable for a reviewable preview. Keep service scope and exclusions adjacent to prices.

Make the form, FAQ and navigation keyboard accessible. Ensure the mobile page reads naturally and the primary CTA remains easy to reach. A working enquiry submission requires a real configured destination; an unconnected preview must not show a successful submission.

### FAQs

**Do we need a working AI prototype?**

This service is designed for teams with an existing prototype, feature or workload. If you are starting earlier, we can discuss what needs to be in place before a production sprint.

**Can you work with our existing engineers?**

Yes. We agree responsibilities with your team and work within the tools, review process and access arrangements set for the project.

**What happens during the first conversation?**

We discuss your current workload, the launch or operating problem, the timeline and the engineering support you need. From there, we recommend an audit, a scoped sprint or an operations engagement.

**Who controls our infrastructure?**

Your cloud and model accounts stay under your control. Project access, documentation and IP terms are agreed before delivery begins.

**How long does a sprint take?**

A bounded workload will typically be scoped for 4–6 weeks. We confirm the schedule after reviewing integrations, access and acceptance requirements.

**Do you offer round-the-clock support?**

The base operations package covers UK business hours. Continuous on-call coverage needs a separate staffed arrangement and quote.

**How do you handle client data?**

We agree data access, processing responsibilities and any subcontractor access before work begins. Your project’s data requirements determine the hosting and delivery arrangements.

**Can we engage you for the audit only?**

Yes. The audit produces recommendations and an implementation plan your team can use. If you proceed to a sprint with us, the audit fee is credited against the agreed sprint price.

Build note: publish FAQs only after the actual service arrangements support these statements. Team location and subcontractor access must be described accurately in procurement and project discussions; do not imply a staffed UK office.

## 5. Launch decisions and quality bar

The page should be built now as a reviewable preview. These specific facts need to be supplied or settled before publication:

1. Final prices, audit credit conditions, actual capacity, coverage hours and response commitments.
2. Actual contracting entity and business disclosures.
3. Working form destination or booking link and a staffed follow-up process.
4. An accurate Privacy Notice for the form, hosting and any analytics.
5. Any real proof assets, founder credentials or client references intended for display.

The initial preview is complete when the hero clearly identifies the service, every CTA works within the preview, all engagement scopes are visible and the mobile page has no clipped content. Publication also requires a real successful enquiry path and verified commercial/business details.
